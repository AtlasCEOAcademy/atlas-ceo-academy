LESSON 01 · FINANCE

Profit vs Cash Flow

Profit is what remains after expenses are deducted from revenue.

Cash flow is the actual money entering and leaving your business.

SOUBLA EXAMPLE: Imagine SOUBLA sells a container for $50,000. Its total costs are $42,000. The accounting profit is $8,000. But if the customer has not paid yet, SOUBLA may have little cash available to pay its bills.

CEO lesson: A profitable company can still fail if it runs out of cash. Always monitor both profit and cash flow.
